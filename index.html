CASE STUDY _ 02

FINFLOW

A Freelance Finance Dashboard - helps freelancers to manage their transaction and invoices.

THE PROBLEM

Freelancers lack a clear, real-time view of their financial health—mixing business and personal transactions, struggling with irregular income, and living in constant fear of miscalculating taxes. Without automation, they waste hours on manual tracking, chase overdue invoices, and face cash flow disruption with every delayed payment.

WHAT I DID

Designed a finance dashboard that empowers freelancers to track money, manage transactions, calculate taxes, and create or send invoices directly to clients—all from one interface.
